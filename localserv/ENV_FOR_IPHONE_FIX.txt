Backend environment variables (set on your host, then redeploy)

# Cookies shared by app.yourdomain.com and api.yourdomain.com  (replace yourdomain.com)
SESSION_COOKIE_DOMAIN=.yourdomain.com
CSRF_COOKIE_DOMAIN=.yourdomain.com
SESSION_COOKIE_SAMESITE=Lax
CSRF_COOKIE_SAMESITE=Lax

# Your real front-end address(es), no trailing slash
CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS=https://app.yourdomain.com
CSRF_TRUSTED_ORIGINS=https://app.yourdomain.com
FRONTEND_URL=https://app.yourdomain.com

# Number of proxies in front of Django (host load balancer = 1; add 1 for Cloudflare etc.)
NUM_PROXIES=1

Front-end: VITE_API_BASE=https://api.yourdomain.com  (and VITE_WS_BASE if you set it)
